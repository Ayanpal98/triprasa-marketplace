A simple, fair starting point

Start with trust. Grow with proof.

The aim is to build a useful local creator network—not a pay-to-praise directory. Participation should be clear, respectful and safe for creators, businesses and customers alike.

✳1. Introduce yourself. Share your content style, language, location and links to work you actually created.
✳2. Match a local story. Creators and businesses agree the scope, schedule, permissions and any compensation before work starts.
✳3. Create with consent. Confirm permission to film people, premises, products and cultural material; disclose gifted or paid partnerships.
✳4. Share and learn. Review audience response honestly. Do not fabricate views, reviews, sales or endorsements.

A few important answers

Clear expectations make better community.

Will creators get paid?

Paid collaborations are a possible part of the future programme, but no rate card, matching service or payment system is active yet. Any future fee, deliverables, usage rights and payment timing should be agreed in writing before work begins.

Can I go live from the website?

Not yet. This page describes a planned live-video format. A production-ready feature would need a streaming provider, creator permissions, moderation, reporting, privacy controls and a clear plan for recording and reuse. In the meantime, creators can indicate interest in hosting sessions.

Can businesses pay for positive reviews?

Triprasa should not sell positive opinions. A business may pay for clearly disclosed sponsored content or a defined production service, but creators must retain the ability to share honest views. Never label a paid promotion as an independent review.

Who can join?

Local creators, photographers, reviewers, video hosts, makers, shop owners and community organisations are welcome to express interest. Any future participation process will need clear eligibility, safety and community standards.
