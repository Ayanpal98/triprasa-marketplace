A more thoughtful marketplace

Local stories.
Shared with respect.

Triprasa is a marketplace concept shaped around a simple belief: local makers should be able to reach new customers without losing the context, credit or control attached to their work.

That starts with listening, asking permission before sharing cultural stories, being transparent about costs, and proving fulfilment works before promising a wider market.
