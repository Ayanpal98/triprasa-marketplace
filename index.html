A marketplace that listens first

Culture lives with the people who carry it.

Tripura’s communities are not one single story. Triprasa is imagined as a place where weavers, bamboo and cane artisans, growers, food producers and independent makers can introduce their work in their own words—with the context they choose to share.

We will not label a design as “traditional” without maker input, flatten distinct communities into one aesthetic, or publish cultural stories without consent. Credit, fair representation and control stay with the people behind the work.
