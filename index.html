A careful kind of growth

Local stories.
Shared with respect.

Triprasa is being shaped around a simple idea: help local makers and small businesses reach new customers without losing the context, credit or control attached to their work.

That means listening first, getting permission before publishing stories or cultural designs, being transparent about costs, and validating fulfilment before promising a wider market.
