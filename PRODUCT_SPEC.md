# Triprasa Product Specification

## Product vision
A community-first marketplace concept connecting Tripura's local makers, indigenous producers and small businesses with new customers while respecting maker attribution, cultural context and seller economics.

## Phase 1 prototype scope
- Responsive landing page and illustrative sample catalogue.
- Search, category filtering and name sorting.
- Local-only demonstration basket and seller-interest form preview.
- Clear disclosure that catalogue entries and prices are illustrative.
- Accessibility foundations: semantic landmarks, skip link, visible focus, labels, status messages and reduced-motion support.

## Out of scope
Backend, database, authentication, seller verification, live inventory, order processing, payment collection, shipping integration, analytics, persistent form storage and real seller data.

## Requirements before a live pilot
- Seller eligibility and onboarding criteria co-designed with participants.
- Consent, attribution, correction and removal procedures for seller profiles and cultural content.
- Accurate product details, origin statements and permission for all images and stories.
- Transparent fees and a method to estimate seller net earnings.
- Packaging and shipping tests, return/refund processes, support ownership and fulfilment responsibilities.
- Privacy notice, data minimisation, access controls and retention policy.
- Applicable legal, tax, consumer-protection and product-specific compliance review.
- Payment-provider integration and reconciliation tested before live orders.

## Pilot measures
Define a baseline and collect evidence before reporting outcomes. Potential measures include onboarding completion, active seller retention, completed orders, cancellations and returns, fulfilment time, repeat purchases, customer support resolution, and seller net earnings after costs. Publish only measured results with clear definitions and consent.

## Acceptance criteria
- The page works on common mobile and desktop widths.
- Search, filters, sorting and basket interactions function locally.
- Seller form does not transmit or persist entered data.
- No fake verification badges, real-seller claims, reviews, live stock claims or unsupported impact metrics appear.
- No live transaction is possible from the prototype.
