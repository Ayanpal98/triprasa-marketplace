# Triprasa Product Specification

## Product vision
Triprasa is a Tripura-rooted, multi-vendor marketplace with a quick-commerce-inspired shopping experience. It should make local discovery, category browsing, search and basket interactions feel fast and familiar while serving indigenous producers, independent makers, local food businesses and everyday sellers.

The product may borrow proven convenience patterns from quick-commerce apps (clear location context, prominent search, category shortcuts, compact product cards, persistent basket access and simple checkout flows). It must retain Triprasa's own brand, local-first positioning and maker/community principles; do not copy another company's brand assets or imply a delivery promise that operations cannot meet.

## Experience principles
- Mobile-first marketplace with search visible near the top.
- Location or service-area selection before any serviceability or delivery estimate is shown.
- Category-led browsing and fast filtering.
- Product cards with clear photos, verified seller identity, unit/pack information, price, stock and add-to-basket action once real listings exist.
- Basket quantity controls and a clear checkout path.
- Seller pages, seller onboarding and a seller/admin operations console in later phases.
- Local maker storytelling and cultural context remain discoverable without interrupting the core shopping journey.

## Current frontend prototype
- Quick-commerce-inspired storefront layout, category shortcuts and prominent search.
- Eight existing illustrative Tripura category previews.
- Local-only demo basket and wishlist.
- Local-only seller-interest form.
- Shopping-area label saved in the current browser only; it does not validate serviceability.
- No actual prices, stock, sellers, delivery ETA, order placement or payment processing.
- Product imagery is illustrative stock photography and is not represented as item-specific seller photography.

## Out of scope until validated
Backend, shared user accounts, seller verification, live inventory, real product pricing, order processing, payment collection, shipping integration, delivery-partner dispatch, analytics, customer support tooling and live seller data.

## Requirements before a live commerce pilot
- Seller eligibility, onboarding and verification criteria co-designed with participants.
- Consent, attribution, correction and removal procedures for seller profiles and cultural content.
- Accurate product details, unit/pack size, origin statements and permission for every image and story.
- Inventory update process and seller-side stock controls.
- Serviceable pin codes/areas and realistic delivery windows based on actual seller and rider capacity.
- Transparent delivery fees, marketplace fees and a method to estimate seller net earnings.
- Packaging and shipping tests, order cancellation, returns/refunds, support ownership and fulfilment responsibilities.
- Privacy notice, data minimisation, access controls, retention policy and security review.
- Applicable legal, tax, consumer-protection and product-specific compliance review.
- Payment-provider integration, refunds, settlement and reconciliation tested before live orders.

## Pilot measures
Define baselines before reporting outcomes. Potential measures include search-to-product engagement, add-to-basket rate, checkout completion, order accuracy, fulfilment time, cancellations, returns, repeat purchases, customer support resolution and seller net earnings after costs. Publish only measured results with clear definitions and consent.

## Acceptance criteria
- Layout works on common mobile, tablet and desktop widths.
- Search, category filters, sorting and demo basket work locally.
- No invented seller names, ratings, reviews, prices, discounts, stock counts or delivery times.
- No unverified product photo is presented as a real seller listing.
- No live transaction is possible from the current prototype.
